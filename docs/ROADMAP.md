# Research roadmap

The original acceptance criteria below remain in force. An end-to-end bounded reference workflow now spans the software interfaces; this is not scientific completion of all phases. See [milestone status](MILESTONES.md) and [workflow guide](RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md).

## Phase 1: Scientific Knowledge Engine
Literature ingestion, ontology, knowledge graph, provenance, epistemic classification. Accept when a sourced claim can be traced to versioned inputs.

## Phase 2: TGA-Ω Engine
State representation, topology, gap detection, transitions. Accept when metric assumptions and small graph examples are reproducible.

## Phase 3: Theory Factory
Conjectures, formalization, symbolic reasoning, hypothesis management. Accept when every candidate has assumptions and falsification conditions.

## Phase 4: Verification Engine
Theorem proving, numerical validation, simulation, adversarial criticism. Accept when invalid candidates fail documented checks.

## Phase 5: Autonomous Research Planner
Select bounded research actions. Accept when decisions are reproducible, calibrated, budgeted, and auditable.

## Phase 6: Blind Research Benchmark
Evaluate withheld problems with leakage controls and independent scoring.

## Phase 7: Experimental Interface
Map predictions to feasible distinguishing experiments, with human approval and explicit budgets.

## Phase 8: Discovery Demonstration
Attempt an open problem; publish provenance, replication, criticism, and failures. No positive discovery is promised.
